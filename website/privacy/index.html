Privacy Policy for KcalGrind

Effective Date: September 13, 2026 • KcalGrind Android Edition

KcalGrind ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, stores, and safeguards your personal and health information when you use KcalGrind on Android.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Account & Profile Data: Email address and authentication identifier (Firebase UID). In guest mode, a randomized anonymous identifier is assigned. We also store nutrition parameters you enter: birth date/age, gender, height, weight, activity level, dietary goals (weight loss, maintenance, muscle gain), and dietary tags/allergies.
  • Nutrition & Health Logs: Logged food items, portion sizes in grams, calorie and macronutrient estimates, water intake logs, and weight check-ins.

B. Beta Tester Email Registration

If you sign up on our website to join the Android closed beta, we collect your Google account email address. This email is used exclusively to register you as an authorized tester on the Google Play closed test track. Beta emails are never used for general marketing, are never shared with third parties, and are retained only for the duration of the closed beta test. You can request immediate removal of your email from the closed test track and our intake records at any time by emailing privacy@kcalgrind.app.

C. Device Permissions & Sensor Data

  • Camera & Meal Photos (Optional): Used when you photograph a meal for nutritional recognition. Photos are processed through Gemini on our secure server proxy to identify ingredients and estimate portion weights. Photos are not saved to your account and are not stored on our servers.
  • Microphone & Voice Logging (Optional): Used when you use the voice logging feature to describe meals. Spoken audio is converted to text via Gemini. Audio recordings are not saved to your account and are not stored on our servers.
  • Health Connect by Android (Optional): If you grant read permissions, KcalGrind reads daily step counts from Android Health Connect to account for daily energy expenditure. KcalGrind only reads step counts and never modifies your Health Connect records. KcalGrind only reads these parameters and never modifies your Health Connect records.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Calculating your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE).
  • Providing automated visual, voice, and natural language food logging and nutritional estimates.
  • Generating constructive coaching insights via Sproutling.
  • Monitoring application performance and crash diagnostics via Firebase Crashlytics (anonymous crash logs without personal identifiers).

3. AI Processing & Third-Party Processors

KcalGrind employs designated third-party service providers to power its features:

  • Google Gemini API: Model inference for analyzing meal photos, parsing voice/natural-language descriptions, and generating Sproutling nutrition guidance under Google Gemini API Terms.
    • Photo & Voice Analysis Endpoints: Meal photos and spoken audio recordings are forwarded via our Cloud Run backend proxy to Google's Gemini API (server.js:357-410). Photos and audio are held strictly in memory during analysis to identify ingredients and estimate portion weights, and are discarded immediately after inference. AI endpoints do not store photo or audio files on our servers, disks, or remote buckets.
    • Sproutling AI Coach Conversations: Each coach message transmits the active conversation history along with your current nutrition parameters (goal, daily target calories, consumed calories today, remaining calories today, and weekly adherence trends) to Google's Gemini API (server.js:1460-1607). When required to answer your query, server tools retrieve today's diary or profile parameters.
    • AI Endpoints Do Not Store Transcripts or Media: Our backend server records only an atomic usage counter in the ai_coach_daily_usage table (server.js:1570) to enforce daily usage limits (5 messages/day free, 50/day Pro). AI endpoints do not store chat transcripts or media files on our servers. Chat transcripts are stored locally on your Android device in SQLite (MessageEntity.kt).
  • Firebase (Google LLC): User authentication management (Firebase Auth) and anonymous crash diagnostic telemetry (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Supabase Inc.: Managed cloud PostgreSQL database hosting remote backups for signed-in users only when manually synchronized via "Sync now".
  • Website Hosting (Cloudflare / Firebase Hosting): Static distribution and CDN delivery of the marketing website, documentation, and static legal pages.
  • Nutritional Datasets: USDA FoodData Central and the Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT 2017) published by the ICMR - National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR-NIN), Hyderabad.
  • Zero Data Broker Sales: We never sell, rent, or monetize personal, dietary, or health information with data brokers or marketing networks.

4. Data Storage, Synchronization & Retention

Data storage and synchronization follow two distinct paths:

  • On-Device Offline Storage (Default): Your food log, daily totals, and chat history are stored directly on your Android device in SQLite (Room) and work completely offline without network access.
  • Manual Cloud Backup ("Sync now"): When you are signed in, cloud backup is strictly manual: you must tap "Sync now" in Profile. There is no automatic background sync. Tapping "Sync now" uploads 9 entities from your local database to your private Supabase cloud account (SyncRepositoryImpl.kt:80-118; server.js:1658-1770):
    1. User Profile (user_profiles)
    2. Nutrition Goals (nutrition_goals)
    3. Meal Logs (meal_logs)
    4. Food Log Items (food_log_items)
    5. Weight Entries (weight_entries)
    6. Water Logs (water_logs)
    7. AI Analyses (ai_analyses)
    8. Conversations (conversations)
    9. Messages (messages)
    Food diaries are stored in the user's private cloud backup when "Sync now" is performed.

    Stored on device only in v1: Custom user-created foods, saved meal templates, and recipes are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to cloud backup.

    Restore on Reinstall: When signing into an existing account on a fresh installation, the app performs a cloud restore (AuthViewModel.kt → SyncRepositoryImpl.kt:restoreCloudBackup), restoring your backed-up profile, nutrition goals, meals, weight, water, and conversations down to your device before opening the Home screen.

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app, backend proxy, and external APIs use TLS 1.3 encryption. Cleartext HTTP traffic is disabled in production release builds.
  • Crash Log Retention: Anonymous Crashlytics logs are retained per Google Firebase standard 90-day retention.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You have complete ownership over your data. You can delete individual logs, reset history, or permanently delete your account and all associated remote records at any time.

  • In-App Deletion: Profile → Delete Account. This wipes your profile, food logs, goals, usage records, and synced backups across all systems.
  • Web Deletion Request: Visit kcalgrind.app/delete-account or email support@kcalgrind.app.

Google Play Subscriptions Note: Deleting your KcalGrind account does NOT automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Subscriptions are managed directly by Google Play. To cancel an active subscription, open the Google Play Store app → Profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → KcalGrind → Cancel subscription.
