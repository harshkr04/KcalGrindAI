KcalGrind • Android Edition • Account & Data Deletion Policy

At KcalGrind, you have complete ownership and control over your personal and health records. You can permanently delete your account and all associated records at any time.

Important: Google Play Subscriptions

Deleting your KcalGrind account does not automatically cancel any active subscription in Google Play. Active subscriptions must be cancelled separately through Google Play: open the Google Play Store app → tap your Profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → select KcalGrind → tap Cancel subscription.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have KcalGrind installed on your Android device, you can delete your account immediately:

  1. Open the KcalGrind app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Review the confirmation summary explaining what data will be wiped.
  5. Tap Permanently Delete and confirm your authentication prompt if requested.

In-app deletion takes effect immediately: your local on-device database, all cloud user-bound records, reminders, and authentication credentials are wiped.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email (Without App Installed)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your Android device, submit an account deletion request by email:

Send email to: support@kcalgrind.app

Subject line: Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email]

Please include:

  • Your registered account email address.
  • A clear statement: "Please permanently delete my KcalGrind account and all associated data."

Email deletion requests are verified and processed within 30 days in compliance with Google Play Developer Policy and global privacy standards.

What Happens When Your Account is Deleted

  • Authentication: Your user identity and Firebase Auth credentials are removed.
  • Cloud Proxy & Server Data: All server-side quota records, chat histories, and usage tokens tied to your user identifier are permanently purged.
  • Local Storage: Your local database on your device (stored meals, custom foods, weight entries) is deleted during in-app deletion.
  • AI Inference: Photo and voice meal analyses are not saved to your account and are not stored on our servers.
